- (direction) Create the first evergreen project spec from the final PRD, UX, architecture, and all other load-bearing project context.
- (decision) SPEC-organizer covers the complete single-user Android validation build; slug organizer is inherited from the source artifact folders.
- (decision) Adopt PRD, addendum, UX EXPERIENCE and DESIGN, architecture spine, and project-context.md as companions because their detailed catalogues, interaction rules, visual system, technical invariants, and delivery gate remain load-bearing.
- (capability) CAP-1: Dispense exactly one suitable micro-task at a time with completion, guilt-free deferral, energy adaptation, rescue, and warm return; success is demonstrated without lists, overdue state, failure counters, or shaming copy.
- (capability) CAP-2: Let the user declare a flexible time pocket and stop or pause freely; success is that dealt work fits the pocket, progress survives interruption, and continuation is never app-initiated.
- (capability) CAP-3: Derive and re-weave each day from Evergreen, Epic, and manual-origin work within time and energy rules; success is deterministic single-card selection with silent recovery from skips and absences.
- (capability) CAP-4: Ship a useful offline Evergreen household library spanning daily, weekly-zone, monthly, and seasonal cadences; success is a fresh-install day with no network, key, model, or typing and the verified non-daily coverage floor.
- (capability) CAP-5: Capture one personal task by typed or on-device dictated text and coarse size without creating a browsable backlog; success is capture in one surface and eligible FIFO dealing within three eligible days.
- (capability) CAP-6: Turn a consented room photo or personal description into sequenced micro-actions through a swappable Slicer; success is useful 3–5 minute steps and calm degradation to Manual Capture whenever slicing is unavailable.
- (capability) CAP-7: Guide organizing projects through purge, empathetic detachment, three equal destinations, quarantine follow-up, and liberated-space feedback; success is purge-first dealing with no destination visually or verbally privileged.
- (capability) CAP-8: Reward progress with private before/after comparisons, a local Transformation Album, and cumulative impact; success is visible progress without streaks, debt, throughput pressure, or negative framing.
- (capability) CAP-9: Offer at most one optional silent ambient invitation per day; success is opt-in, content-free, non-urgent notification behavior that cannot escalate or expose pending work.
- (capability) CAP-10: Keep user data local while enforcing per-scan consent, face/person refusal before upload, upload minimization, closed egress, secure BYOK custody, deletion, export, and restore; success is no developer-held data and a full authoritative export/import round trip.
- (capability) CAP-11: Produce local validation evidence for sustained use, overwhelm reduction, Epic progress, and photo-loop adoption while guarding against marathon, guilt, and notification pressure; success is a readable four-week export containing the defined measures and counter-metrics.
- (constraint) The validation build is single-user Android, local-first, backend-free, Flutter 3.47 and Dart 3.13, with a pure-Dart functional core and an imperative Flutter/adapters shell.
- (constraint) Zero cognitive load and anti-shaming are product invariants: no pending-work list, overdue state, streak, undone count, pressure to continue, or surfaced failure; only the two PRD-declared genesis/template exceptions may enumerate choices.
- (constraint) Domain state is replayable from immutable pool facts and an append-only event log; daily plans and sessions are derived deterministically under the architecture spine.
- (constraint) The app has one sealed egress module and no analytics, crash-reporting, remote-config, or other network SDK; only the three Slicer payload shapes may leave the device.
- (constraint) Photos require per-scan consent and on-device person detection; scan files are destroyed on every terminal path, and voice audio is never stored, exported, or transmitted.
- (constraint) BYOK is the only usable release Slicer path; provider selection is compile-time allowlisted by dated written no-training terms, credentials are non-exportable, and no account or proxy exists.
- (constraint) Every implementation unit must pass flutter test, dart format --set-exit-if-changed ., and flutter analyze before it is done.
- (decision) Non-goals: multi-user coordination, iOS and other platforms, backend/accounts/sync, Managed or production Local slicing, subscription billing, GTD-style browsing/editing, calendar/deadline semantics, social/gamified pressure, and automatic sharing.
- (assumption) Treat the spec as a living evergreen contract for the project while its present scope remains the four-week validation build; later scope changes append to the memlog and preserve CAP IDs.

- (question) Should Manual Capture accept non-spatial or date-bearing work, and what copy explains the boundary without shaming the user?
- (question) Which unresolved UX copy, onboarding, settings IA, accessibility, permission-denial, theme-selection, and before/after presentation decisions must be closed before their corresponding surfaces are implemented?
