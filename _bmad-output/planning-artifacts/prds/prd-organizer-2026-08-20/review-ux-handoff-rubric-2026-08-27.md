# PRD Quality Review — Anti-Overwhelm Mobile Task Organizer (UX blocker-resolution pass, 2026-08-27)

Reviewed against `.agents/skills/bmad-prd/assets/prd-validation-checklist.md`. Scope of review: the 2026-08-27 "UX blocker-resolution handoff" pass (§1.1 E2 removal, FR-4, FR-11, FR-16, FR-27, FR-31, FR-32, §7, §9, §10.1, addendum A5, Changelog), with the rest of `prd.md` + `addendum.md` read in full to catch contradictions the pass may have introduced.

## Overall verdict

The pass itself is clean and well-cascaded: E2's removal is complete inside this document (register, FR-11, §9, §10.1 all agree), the rewritten FRs are among the most testable in the PRD, and every touched rule was carried through to Glossary, §7, §9 and §10.1. What is at risk is one seam the pass created and two it sharpened: FR-4's new Sunday sequencing states resolution semantics that contradict SM-2's persistence rule on the PRD's primary metric instrument; the §7 keyboard-free floor now collides with the genesis surface the pass gave a "typed entry" recommendation to; and the Dispenser's chrome — which the pass added two elements to — has no canonical inventory anywhere. Nothing found is critical; the document remains decision-grade for UX/architecture/stories once the Sunday seam is fixed.

## Decision-readiness — strong

The pass is decision-dense in the good way: every ruling is stated as a ruling with its reasoning and its rejected alternative on the record. FR-4 kills late-day decay with the reason ("it would shrink the pool precisely during the app's real usage window and would turn an ambient, optional check-in into a necessary correction"), and addendum A5 records the same ruling for downstream readers. FR-16 reduces a fuzzy visibility question to a formula — "Visibility is *enabled and permission not refused*" — that an implementer can encode directly. OQ-11's closure names what was refused and why ("rejecting it would shame the user and detecting it reliably is impossible"), and OQ-13's closure states what survives the ruling ("recognition *unavailability* shows nothing at all, while a *refused permission* carries a settings reactivation row"). The Changelog even records the debt this pass creates *outside* the document ("SPEC.md and ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md must drop E2 from their sources lines") rather than silently assuming it will happen. Open questions that remain (OQ-1, OQ-5, OQ-6, OQ-7) are genuinely open and routed to a named owner; none is rhetorical. No findings.

## Substance over theater — strong

No furniture detected, and the pass added none. One real user (Sergio) carries every UJ; non-users are named to keep boundary decisions honest rather than to look thorough. Constraints carry product-specific bounds everywhere (≤ 2 s cold start, ≤ 500 ms re-filter, ≥ 28 eligible Focus Chunk entries, 4-week window). The differentiator claims are anchored to a researched landscape (A1) with honesty flags where research failed ("Unverified: the research search timed out" — Nipto). The pass's own contributions are load-bearing rather than decorative: FR-4's "resolved = gone for the day" and FR-16's dead-camera-icon rationale ("a dead camera icon sitting on the primary surface is pending work in the one place the user lives") both apply §1.1 principle 2 to new surface decisions instead of restating it. No findings.

## Strategic coherence — strong

The thesis (one action, zero guilt, anti-marathon) demonstrably drives the pass's rulings rather than being decorated by them: the check-in was rewritten *away* from permanent furniture precisely because a permanent energy control reads as a gate; the genesis restructure removed an exception (E2) by restoring "one recommended action plus a way out" arithmetic instead of widening the register; camera-entry visibility exists so principle 2 is not violated by an icon. Success metrics still validate the thesis (SM-2 felt overwhelm is the premise test) with counter-metrics named (SM-C1–C3), and the pass protected the metric instrument's character (SM-2 "never notified", "persisting until answered"). One slow-burn coherence risk — Dispenser chrome accumulation — is filed under Downstream usability with the coherence angle noted there. No separate findings.

## Done-ness clarity — adequate

The base standard here is high: nearly every FR consequence in this document is testable, and the pass's rewrites maintain that (FR-4's dismissal semantics, FR-16's visibility formula, FR-27's "no validation, no refusal" are all directly testable). The verdict drops to adequate because the pass introduced one direct semantic conflict on the primary metric's collection surface and left two sequencing gaps an implementer reading a single FR would build wrong.

### Findings

- **high** SM-2 resolution semantics conflict (FR-4 vs §8 SM-2 / §10.1) — FR-4's new Sunday clause says the self-report "holds the check-in's slot first; once it resolves — **answered or dismissed** — the check-in may take the same slot", and adds "Each is one tap, **nothing nags after**". SM-2's own definition (§8, twice, and the §10.1 instrument row) says it is "an ambient question in the Dispenser on the first opening from Sunday onward, **persisting until answered that week**" — dismissal is not a resolution for SM-2, and it does nag (ambiently) by design. As written, an implementer of FR-4 alone builds a dismissible Sunday self-report that never returns (losing the week's data point); an implementer of SM-2 alone builds a question the check-in can never take the slot from on a dismissal. The "may take the same slot" hedging verb compounds it — a spec should not leave the slot handoff to implementer discretion. *Fix:* in FR-4, scope "answered or dismissed" to the check-in only; state SM-2 resolves by answer alone and say deterministically what the check-in does while SM-2 persists unanswered (deferred to the next opening, or shown beside it — pick one).
- **medium** UJ-4's Sunday narrative contradicts FR-4's Sunday rule (§2.3 UJ-4 vs FR-4) — UJ-4 opens "Sergio taps the 1-tap energy check-in" at what the journey itself calls "a low-battery Sunday" first opening, with no weekly self-report in the narrative. Under the new rule, the first thing a Sunday first opening holds is SM-2 — which persists until answered — so the journey as written depicts the one sequencing the new FR-4 forbids. FR-4 claims "Realizes UJ-4"; the realized and the realization now disagree. *Fix:* rewrite UJ-4's opening to show the Sunday order (self-report resolves, then the check-in), or move the journey to a non-Sunday day.
- **medium** Persisting SM-2 past Sunday meets the daily check-in undefined (FR-4 / §8 SM-2) — SM-2 persists "until answered that week", so an unanswered Sunday surfaces again on Monday's (or Wednesday's) first opening. FR-4's yields-rule is scoped to "On Sundays" only: nothing says whether a still-pending SM-2 holds the check-in's slot on those days, coexists with it, or is ignored. The two surfaces share the Dispenser and the first-opening moment; the composition is unwritten. *Fix:* add one FR-4 consequence covering any first opening where a prior week's SM-2 is still pending.
- **medium** Keyboard-free floor vs the genesis surface (§7, §10.1 vs FR-11, FR-32) — §7 states "**No keyboard is required on any user surface**" and §10.1's row promises "every surface asking the user for writing has a wordless or spoken route". The pass made the genesis surface's "one recommended action" **typed entry** (FR-11), and FR-32 forbids dictation "on the Manual Capture surface and nowhere else in the build". The genesis surface therefore asks for writing with no spoken route on it; the wordless route (photo) is a *different path on a different entrance*, not a route on that surface. A reader of §7 + FR-32 building FR-11's surface faces a direct instruction conflict. (Pre-existing in part — the floor predates the pass — but the pass sharpened the genesis surface's typing commitment without reconciling the floor.) *Fix:* either scope §7's floor to path level ("every *task-genesis goal* has a wordless or spoken route", with the photo path named as the wordless project route), or name the genesis surface's exception explicitly in §7 and §10.1.
- **low** UJ-4 "skips a task three times in a row" vs FR-5's trigger (§2.3 UJ-4 vs FR-5) — FR-5's automatic heuristic fires on declines "on 3 different calendar days" and explicitly does not count same-day declines; UJ-4's "skips a task three times in a row" reads as a same-session triple-skip triggering Rescue Mode — behavior FR-5 forbids. Pre-existing, not introduced by this pass, but UJ-4 is FR-4/FR-5's realization target and was left untouched while its neighbours were rewritten. *Fix:* "skips a task three days running" or equivalent.

## Scope honesty — strong

Omissions stay explicit and two-kind (§5.1 deferred vs §5.2 philosophy-excluded), and the pass kept the discipline: E2's removal is *argued* in the register's terms (A-slim genesis restores literal principle-1 arithmetic) rather than silently deleted, and the Changelog entry records both what left the document and what downstream documents must now change. §10.2 assumptions each carry their predicted failure path and the series that would detect it — the new pass added no fresh assumptions because it closed questions rather than opening them (OQ-3 surviving half, OQ-11, OQ-13 all closed with dated rulings). Open-items density is now low and appropriate for a build-ready PRD: 4 open OQs, all routed. No findings.

## Downstream usability — adequate

The single-sourcing discipline is exemplary — `Values: §10.1` everywhere, glossary-anchored vocabulary, IDs contiguous and cross-references that resolve (verified FR-1..32, UJ-1..5, SM/SM-C, OQ-1..13, E1). Two things keep this from strong: the addendum was not cascaded for two closures this pass made, and the surface downstream UX needs most — the Dispenser — must now be assembled from seven separate sections.

### Findings

- **medium** No canonical Dispenser surface inventory (FR-1/FR-4/FR-11/FR-16/FR-27/FR-31, §8 SM-2) — after this pass the Dispenser carries: the card with Done/Skip (FR-1–3), a direct one-tap camera entry (FR-16, new), "the Dispenser's single quiet affordance" opening the genesis surface (FR-11, new phrasing), Manual Capture "reachable from the Dispenser in one tap" (FR-27), the energy check-in at every day's first opening (FR-4, now daily), SM-2's ambient question on Sundays/pending weeks, the one-time curation strip (FR-31), and the Warm Return screen after absence (FR-6). No section enumerates the composed surface or the stacking rules between its ambient elements; `bmad-ux` must reconcile seven citations to draw one screen, and the pass never choreographed, e.g., the strip's "after first run" timing against the check-in's first-opening slot, or Warm Return against both. Note also FR-11's claim "the paths never share a surface" is literally false at entrance level — camera entry and the quiet affordance are both Dispenser entrances; it only holds at interaction-surface level. *Fix:* one short "Dispenser composition" subsection (or an expanded Glossary entry) enumerating persistent chrome vs first-opening ambients vs one-time elements, with the stacking order — this is exactly what the UX spine needs next.
- **low** §10.1 unified row contradicts FR-16's row-existence rule (§10.1 vs FR-16/FR-32) — the row says "a refused permission removes the entry until reactivated in settings (**the row exists only while there is something to reactivate**)". True for the microphone (FR-32: "a phone with recognition available and permission granted shows no row at all"), false for the camera: FR-16's "single settings row owns both the disable toggle and the reactivation" means the camera row exists whenever the entry is visible — i.e., always. The unification is in the refused→settings-reversal pattern, not in row existence, and the row's parenthetical flattens that. Related unspecified edge: a permission revoked in *system* settings after being granted (FR-16 describes only refusal "at first use"). *Fix:* split the parenthetical per entry (mic: row only while refused; camera: row always present, carrying disable + reactivation), and state that post-grant revocation follows the refusal rule.
- **low** Addendum not cascaded for this pass's OQ closures (addendum A12.6, A13 vs §9) — A12.6 still says of non-spatial errands "whether such work belongs in the product at all is **OQ-11, unresolved**", and A13 still carries "**Deliberately left open.** What the app does when on-device Spanish recognition is missing (OQ-13)" with the counter-argument presented as live. §9 closed both on 2026-08-27 (OQ-11: spatial framing, no validation, no refusal; OQ-13: absent-and-silent, no pointer, closed at `bmad-ux`). A downstream reader working from the addendum — which §0 explicitly directs depth-seekers to — will treat two settled rulings as open. *Fix:* one-line dated closure notes in A12.6 and A13, matching the A5 treatment this pass already applied.

## Shape fit — strong

Chain-top solo validation build (§0 names its downstream consumers), and the shape matches: UJs with a named protagonist are load-bearing (each maps to FRs that cite back), rigor is calibrated to one user on one phone, and depth is offloaded to the addendum rather than inflating the body. The pass respected the shape — it tightened an exception register instead of growing it, and closed OQs instead of adding them. Brownfield concerns don't apply. No findings.
